Every weather API below is currently online (verified by our daily health checks).

- **Best for:** Hobby projects, prototypes, anything

- **Free tier:** 60 calls/min, 1,000 calls/day

### 3. National Weather Service (NWS)
- **Free tier:** Completely free, no key
- **Best for:** US-based applications
- **Note:** US coverage only, but 100% free and government-backed

### 4. WeatherAPI.com
- **Free tier:** 1,000,000 calls/month
- **Best for:** High-volume applications
- **Bonus:** Includes astronomy data (sunrise/sunset)

### 5. 7Timer!
- **Free tier:** Completely free, no key

- **Note:** Lightweight, designed for astronomy apps
